`.iq-threat-bar` is used at the left edge of a table cell or list item to indicate via colour the threat level of the information to follow. There are two scales to choose from: threat level and policy threat level. The names and colour variables are located in the `/scss/_variables.scss` file. Both are represented in the table below. Other threat indicators such as threat indicators and threat backgrounds have similar purposes but different applications. Typically the threat level is set programmatically: `<span class="iq-threat-bar iq-threat-bar--policy-level-{{policy.threatLevel}}"></span>` and `<span class="iq-threat-bar iq-threat-bar--critical">`
